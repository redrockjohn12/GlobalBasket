FAQ

Questions, Answered

What do I send you?

Find your product in one of our listed stores, take a screenshot showing the product details, and send the screenshot to our WhatsApp number: +268 7953 3390.

When do I pay?

We first check the order and send you the applicable quote. Once you approve the quote and make payment, we confirm the payment and proceed with the order.

What happens after I pay?

We confirm that your payment has been received, place the approved order, and keep you informed as the order progresses.

How does the 25% fee work?

Global Basket Eswatini charges a 25% service fee on the applicable order subtotal. For AliExpress and Alibaba, the product amount plus applicable shipping is the subtotal before the 25% fee is added.

Do you provide tracking?

Yes. We track your order and communicate important updates throughout the process until you receive your order.
